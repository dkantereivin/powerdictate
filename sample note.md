* Incentive spirometry Q1H while awake; chest physiotherapy; respiratory therapy consult for scheduled oscillating positive expiratory pressure (Acapella) flutter valve.

#### 2. Renal / Acute Kidney Injury (AKI)

* **Nephrotoxic Discontinuation & Avoidance:**
* Absolute avoidance of NSAIDs (cancel PRN ketorolac and celecoxib permanently).
* Withhold nephrotoxic perioperative agents and hold home SGLT2 inhibitor (empagliflozin) and aldosterone antagonist (spironolactone).
* Hold Sacubitril/Valsartan (Entresto) temporarily given acute doubling of creatinine and borderline blood pressure (MAP 75–80 mmHg).


* **Fluid & Hemodynamic Management:**
* Avoid large unguided crystalloid boluses given underlying systolic dysfunction and bilateral pulmonary infiltrates.
* Administer a controlled small fluid trial: 250–500 mL Plasmalyte over 2 hours; evaluate stroke volume variation or bedside echocardiography (inferior vena cava collapsibility) to guide fluid responsiveness.
* If MAP falls below 65 mmHg, transfer to Medical ICU for norepinephrine infusion to maintain renal perfusion pressure.


* **Monitoring:** Strict intake and output via indwelling urinary catheter; repeat BMP and ionized calcium in 12 hours (monitor potassium closely; current K⁺ 5.3 mmol/L). Have calcium gluconate and insulin/dextrose at bedside if potassium exceeds 5.5 mmol/L.
